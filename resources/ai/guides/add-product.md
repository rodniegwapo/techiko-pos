1. In the sidebar, open **Products → Items**.
2. Click **Create Product** (top right). If the button is greyed out, the free plan's 10-product limit is reached; open **Servicing payment** to subscribe.
3. Fill in **Product Name** and **Price**. These are required.
4. Optional: pick a **Category**, enter the **Cost** (used for profit reports), and enter or **Generate** a **SKU** and **Barcode**.
5. Choose the **Sold Type** (for example per piece).
6. Optional: set the **Display style** (image, color or text) shown on the POS screen.
7. Click **Create Product**. The product is added to the store you are working in.
