1. In the sidebar, open **Finance → Bank, loans & equipment** and stay on **Bank & e-wallets**.
2. Click **Add account**. Enter the **Name** (e.g. "BDO savings", "GCash"), the **Type**, and optionally the **Account number** and which payment channel customers pay into it through.
3. Enter the **Current balance** and **As of** date, then save.
4. Later, click **Update balance** on the account, enter the new **Balance** and **As of** date, and save.

The latest balance of each account is used in the balance sheet.
