title: Redeem a customer's loyalty points at checkout
page: sales.index
requires: sales.index
keywords: redeem points, use points, loyalty points, rewards, gamitin points, points discount
---
1. On the **Sales** screen, switch to **Loyal** and pick the customer.
2. Add the products to the order.
3. Next to **Loyalty redemption** (in the totals area), click the gift icon (**Apply loyalty redemption**).
4. In **Loyalty redemption**, check the customer's **Balance** and the **Max redeem** for this sale, enter how many points to use, and save. The peso value is taken off the total.
5. Take payment as usual.

The point value, minimum and maximum per sale are shown under **Loyalty Program → Program Rules**.
