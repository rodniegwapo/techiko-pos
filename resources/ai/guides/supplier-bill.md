1. In the sidebar, open **Finance → Supplier bills**.
2. Click **Record bill**.
3. Pick the **Supplier** (click **New** to add one, with how many days you have to pay: **Pays bills within (days)**).
4. Under **What is it for?**, choose **Stock to sell** or **An expense** (then pick the **Expense category**).
5. Enter the **Amount**, **Bill / invoice no.** and **Bill date**. The **Due date** fills from the supplier's terms; change it if needed.
6. Optional: **Store (optional)** and **Notes**. Click **Save**.

The bill shows under **Coming up** or **Overdue bills** until it is paid. The assistant can record a bill or add a supplier for you after you confirm.
