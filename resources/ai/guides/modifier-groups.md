1. In the sidebar, open **Products → Items** and click **Modifiers**.
2. Click **Create Modifier Group**.
3. Enter the **Group name** (e.g. Size, Sugar level, Add-ons).
4. Under **Cashier picks**, choose **One** or **Several** (and **At most**, if there is a limit). Tick **Required** if the cashier must choose.
5. Under **Options**, add each one with its **Name** and what it **Adds to price** (and **Adds to cost**).
6. Under **Products with this group**, pick the products it applies to, then click **Save**.

At the POS, the cashier is asked to choose the options when adding those products.
