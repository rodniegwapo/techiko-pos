1. On the **Sales** screen, add the products to the order.
2. Next to **Order Discount**, click the discount icon (**Apply Order Discount**).
3. In the mandatory discount field (**Select PWD, Senior, Student, etc.**), choose **PWD** or **Senior**.
4. Save. The discount shows under **Order Discount** and the **Total** goes down.
5. Take payment as usual.

The PWD/Senior rates are managed under **Products → Mandatory Discounts**.
