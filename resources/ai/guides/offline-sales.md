1. While online, on the **Sales** screen click **Sync for offline**. This saves products and prices in the browser so the POS works offline.
2. When the internet is down, sell as usual and click **Save offline sale** instead of Proceed Payment. The sale stays in this browser.
3. Open **Offline sales** in the sidebar to see the **Pending offline sales**.
4. Click **Accept** on each sale to keep (or **Reject** to drop it).
5. Accepted sales sync automatically once the internet is back.

Use the same browser and device until the sales are synced; offline sales are saved in that browser only.
