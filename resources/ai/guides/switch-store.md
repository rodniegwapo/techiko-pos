title: Switch the store you work in
page: dashboard
requires: inventory.locations.switch
keywords: switch store, change branch, switch location, lipat store
---
1. At the top of the page, click the store name badge.
2. Choose **Switch Location** and pick the store.
3. Sales, products and stock now show for that store. This only changes it for you, not for other staff.
