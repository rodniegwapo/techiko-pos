1. In the sidebar, open **Finance → Bank, loans & equipment** and go to **Loans**.
2. Click **Record loan**. Enter the **Lender** (e.g. Landbank, a relative), the **Amount borrowed** and **Received on** date.
3. Optional: **Interest rate per year** and **Due date**. Save.
4. When you pay it, click **Repay** on the loan. Enter the **Principal** part, the **Interest** part and the **Date**, then save.

**Loans still owed** at the top shows what is left to pay.
