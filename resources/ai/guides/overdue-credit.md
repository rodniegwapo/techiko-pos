1. In the sidebar, open **Credit Management**.
2. Use the **Status** filter and choose **Overdue** (or **At Limit** for customers who reached their limit).
3. Each customer shows their **Credit Limit**, **Balance** and **Available** credit.
4. Click **View details** to see their **Outstanding Invoices**, **Total Overdue** and **Payment History**, and to **Record Payment**.
