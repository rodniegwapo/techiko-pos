1. In the sidebar, open **Users**.
2. Click **Add User**. (If you see **Plan user limit reached**, your plan has no free user slots; see **Servicing payment**.)
3. Enter the **Full Name** and **Email Address**.
4. Choose the **Role** (e.g. cashier, supervisor, manager). The **Role Permissions** preview shows what they can do.
5. If asked, pick who they report to (e.g. **Assign Supervisor** for a cashier).
6. Enter a **Password** and **Confirm Password**, then save.

Give them their email and password so they can log in. Set a PIN for them if they will approve voids (see "Set a user's PIN").
