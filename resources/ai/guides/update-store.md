1. In the sidebar, open **Inventory → Locations**.
2. Find the store (use **Search locations...**) and choose **Edit** from its actions.
3. Change the **Location Name**, **Location Code**, **Address** or contact details. Untick **Active Location** to stop using it.
4. Save.
