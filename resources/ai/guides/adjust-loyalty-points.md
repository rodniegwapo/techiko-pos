page: loyalty.index
requires: loyalty.index, loyalty.adjust-points

---
1. In the sidebar, open **Loyalty Program** and go to **Customer Management**.
2. Find the customer and click **Adjust points**.
3. Under **Adjustment Type**, choose **Add Points** or **Deduct Points**.
4. Enter the **Points Amount** and a **Reason**. The **New Total** is previewed.
5. Save.
