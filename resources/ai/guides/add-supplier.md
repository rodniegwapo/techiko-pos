1. In the sidebar, open **Finance → Supplier bills**.
2. Click **Suppliers**, then **Add supplier**.
3. Enter the **Name**. Optional: **Contact person**, **Phone**, **Email** and **Notes**.
4. Set **Pays bills within (days)**, how many days you have to pay their bills (it starts at 30).
5. Click **Save**.

You can also add a supplier while recording a bill: click **New** next to **Supplier**. The assistant can add a supplier for you after you confirm.
