title: Assign a supervisor to a user
page: users.index
requires: users.index, users.update, supervisors.available

---
1. In the sidebar, open **Users**.
2. Find the user and click **Edit user** on their row.
3. Pick the person in the assign field. It is **Assign Supervisor** for a cashier, **Assign Manager** for a supervisor, and **Assign Admin** for a manager.
4. Save.

Click **User Hierarchy** on the Users page to see who reports to whom, and who has no supervisor yet.
