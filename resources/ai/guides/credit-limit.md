1. In the sidebar, open **Credit Management**.
2. Find the customer and click **Edit credit limit** on their row (or open them and click **Edit Credit Limit**).
3. In **Credit Settings**, tick **Enable Credit for this Customer**.
4. Enter the **Credit Limit** (the most they may owe) and the **Payment Terms (Days)** (how many days they have to pay).
5. Save. The customer can now buy using **Credit** on the Sales screen, up to the limit.
