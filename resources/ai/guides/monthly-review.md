1. In the sidebar, open **Finance → Monthly reviews**.
2. Pick a month to read it: **Sales**, **Gross profit** and **Net profit** compared with the month before, **What went well**, **What needs attention** and **Recommended actions**.
3. A review is written automatically at the start of each month. To write last month's now, or redo one after recording more expenses, use **Write it again**.

When the AI is set up, an AI summary of the month appears at the top.
