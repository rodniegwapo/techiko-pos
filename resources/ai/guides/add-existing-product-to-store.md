key: add-existing-product-to-store
title: Sell an existing product in another store
page: products.index
requires: products.index
keywords: add existing to store, attach product, product in other store, other branch
---
1. Switch to the store that should sell the product (see "Switch the store you work in").
2. In the sidebar, open **Products → Items**.
3. Click **Add existing to store** (only shown when the business has more than one store).
4. Pick the products and confirm.
