page: inventory.products

1. In the sidebar, open **Inventory → Products**.
2. Check that the store shown (**Viewing inventory for: …**) is the one you want; switch the store at the top if needed.
3. Search by product name, SKU or barcode.
4. Each row shows the quantity in this store (**Qty (store)**), **On hand**, **Value** and **Avg cost**.
5. Click **View details** for more on one product.
