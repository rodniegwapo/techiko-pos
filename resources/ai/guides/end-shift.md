Each cashier has their own shift; several can be on shift at one store.

1. In the sidebar, open **Cash & wallet → Today's drawer**.
2. Under **My shift**, click **End my shift**.
3. Count the cash in your drawer and enter it. Add any cash expenses you paid from the drawer during the shift. Click **End my shift**. No manager's PIN is needed.
4. If the count is off from Expected, the shift shows **Needs review** and a manager looks into it on **Cash & wallet → Shifts & counts**. You can start a new shift meanwhile (unless the store turned that off in Settings).

Made a mistake? **Reopen** under My shift opens it again; say why. The first count stays in the history, and ending it again adds a new count. An approved shift can't be reopened: record a correction in **Cash in & out**.

Managers: every cashier's shift is in the table on **Today's drawer**, with **Approve** and **Recount**; past ones are on **Shifts & counts**. What each person took in, by where it went (cash drawer, GCash, bank, card), is on **Where money is → By cashier**. When every cashier has ended their shift, **End the day** closes the store's day; reopening the day also needs a reason.
