1. In the sidebar, open **Cash & wallet → Money movement**, check the **Business date** and click **Load**.
2. Count the cash in the drawer. Click **Submit counted cash**, enter the amount and click **Save counted cash**. The page compares it with the **Expected** cash.
3. Click **End Shift**.
4. Choose what to do with the counted cash: **Cash out now** (take it out of the drawer) or **Save as opening cash** (leave it as tomorrow's starting cash).

Made a mistake? **Reopen Shift** opens it again, but only for the user who closed it.
