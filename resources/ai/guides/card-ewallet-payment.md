1. On the **Sales** screen, add the products to the order.
2. Under **Payment method**, choose **Card**, or open **Other** and choose **E-wallet** or **Bank**.
3. Click **Choose** and pick the card type, e-wallet (e.g. GCash) or bank. You can type a new one if it is not in the list.
4. For e-wallet or bank, type the **Reference no.** from the customer's receipt (optional).
5. Click **Proceed Payment** (or **Checkout** on the Modern screen).

The money received through each channel is tracked under **Cash & wallet → Where money is**.
