1. In the sidebar, open **Users**.
2. Find the user and click **Set PIN** on their row.
3. Enter a **PIN** of 4 to 6 digits and repeat it in **Confirm PIN**.
4. Click **Save PIN**.

Managers and admins use their PIN to approve voids; each PIN must be different within the business.
