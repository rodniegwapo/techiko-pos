1. In the sidebar, open **Void Logs**.
2. Each row shows the **Product**, **Reason**, **Cashier** and **Sale ref**.
3. Click **View details** to see the date and time, the **Amount** and who approved it (**Approved by**).
4. Use the search box to find a product, reason or cashier.
