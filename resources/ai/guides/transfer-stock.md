requires: inventory.products, inventory.transfer
keywords: transfer stock, move stock, lipat stock, ilipat, other branch, ibang branch, between stores, warehouse to store
---
1. In the sidebar, open **Inventory → Products**.
2. Click **Transfer Stock** (or **Transfer stock** on a product's row).
3. Choose **From** (source location) and **To** (destination location).
4. Search and add the products, and type the **Quantity** of each (it can't be more than **Available** at the source).
5. Optional: add **Notes (Optional)**.
6. Click **Transfer Inventory**. Stock goes down at the source and up at the destination.
