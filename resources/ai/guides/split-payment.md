1. On the **Sales** screen, add the products to the order.
2. Under **Payment method**, open **Other** and choose **Split payment**.
3. For each part, choose the method (Cash, Card, E-wallet, Bank or Credit) and type the amount. Add a reference no. for e-wallet or bank parts.
4. Click **Add payment** for more parts (up to 4). The panel shows what is **Remaining** and any **Change**.
5. When the parts add up to the total, click **Proceed Payment** (or **Checkout** on the Modern screen).

Only the cash part can be more than the total (the extra is given as change).
