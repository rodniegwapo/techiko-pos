1. In the sidebar, open **Cash & wallet → Cash in & out**.
2. Click **+ Cash in** (**Added float** or **Correction**) or **− Cash out** (**Owner took cash** or **Correction**).
3. Enter the **Amount** and the **Date**. For **Owner took cash**, choose whether it was **Taken from** the **Cash drawer** or a payment channel.
4. Optional: **Notes**. Click **Save**.

Money moved into or out of GCash, a bank or a card terminal goes under **Move money**; it changes that channel's balance, not the cash drawer. Expenses, supplier payments and other income paid in cash show up here automatically. A shift that has ended must be reopened on **Today's drawer** before adding lines for that day.
