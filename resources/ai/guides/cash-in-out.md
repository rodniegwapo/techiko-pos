1. In the sidebar, open **Cash & wallet → Money movement**.
2. Click **Add entry** and choose **Cash in** (e.g. adding change/float), **Cash out** (e.g. the owner taking money) or **Other entry**.
3. Enter the **Amount**, the **Movement date** and, for cash out, where it was taken from (**Withdraw from**: the cash register or a payment channel).
4. Optional: **Notes**. Click **Save**.

Expenses, supplier payments and other income paid in cash are added here automatically. A closed shift must be reopened before adding entries for that day.
