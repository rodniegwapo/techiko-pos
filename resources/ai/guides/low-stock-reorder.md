**See what is low:**
1. In the sidebar, open **Inventory → Dashboard**.
2. Check the **Low Stock Alert** list.

**Set when a product counts as low:**
1. Open **Products → Items** and choose **Edit** on the product.
2. Make sure **Track stock** is on.
3. Set the **Low stock level** (the quantity at which it shows as low) and save.
