1. In the sidebar, open **Inventory → Stock Adjustments**.
2. Find the adjustment waiting for approval and click **View details**.
3. Check the **Adjustment Items** (System Qty, Actual Qty, Adjustment) and the **Value Impact**.
4. Click **Approve** to apply it to the stock, or **Reject** to turn it down.

You can also use **Approve** / **Reject** directly on the row.
