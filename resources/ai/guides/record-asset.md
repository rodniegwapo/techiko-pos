1. In the sidebar, open **Finance → Accounts, loans & assets** and go to **Equipment & assets**.
2. Click **Record asset**.
3. Enter **What is it?**, the **Kind**, the **Cost** and the date it was **Bought on**.
4. Enter its **Useful life (months)** so its value goes down over time; leave it empty for things that don't lose value (e.g. land).
5. Save. **Equipment & assets (value now)** shows the cost less depreciation.

When you sell or throw it away, edit it and fill in **Sold or thrown away on**.
