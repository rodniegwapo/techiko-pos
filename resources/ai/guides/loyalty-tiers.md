1. In the sidebar, open **Loyalty Program** and go to **Tier Management**.
2. Click **Add New Tier**.
3. Enter the **Tier Name** (can't be changed later) and **Display Name** (e.g. Gold).
4. Set the **Points Multiplier** (e.g. 1.25 earns 25% more points) and the **Spending Threshold** (lifetime spending to reach this tier).
5. Optional: **Tier Color**, **Sort Order** and **Description**. Keep it **Active**.
6. Save.

How points are earned and redeemed is shown under **Program Rules**.
