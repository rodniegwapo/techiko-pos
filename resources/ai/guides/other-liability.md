1. In the sidebar, open **Finance → Accounts, loans & assets** and go to **Other liabilities**.
2. Click **Record amount owed**.
3. Enter **What is it?** (e.g. "VAT for September", "Deposit from Santos catering"), the **Kind** and the **Amount**.
4. Enter **Owed since** and, if known, **Due (optional)**. Save.
5. Once it is paid, edit it and fill in **Settled on (once paid)**.
