title: Void a completed sale
page: sales-history.index
requires: sales-history.index, sales-history.void
keywords: void sale, cancel sale, refund, void receipt, ibalik, cancel benta, mali ang benta, void transaction
---
1. In the sidebar, open **Sales History** and find the sale (search the **Invoice #**).
2. Click the sale to open it.
3. Click **Void receipt**.
4. In **Void Receipt**, enter the **Pin** and a **Reason**, then confirm. A cashier needs a manager's or admin's PIN; managers and admins use their own.
5. The sale shows **This sale was voided** and is no longer counted in sales. The void is listed in **Void Logs**.

A void can't be undone, so check the invoice number first.
