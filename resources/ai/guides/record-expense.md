1. In the sidebar, open **Finance → Expenses**.
2. Click **Record Expense**.
3. Enter the **Amount**, the **Date** and a **Description** (e.g. "October rent", "Meralco bill").
4. Choose the **Category** (click **Manage** to add one).
5. Choose **Paid from**: **Cash register** (cash taken from the store's register), Bank transfer, E-wallet, Card or Other. Cash register expenses also show as cash out in the cash count.
6. Optional: **Payee**, **Store**, **Reference no.**, a photo of the **Receipt**, and **Notes**.
7. Save.

To have it come back every month or week, set **Repeat** (see "Set up a recurring expense"). The assistant can also record an expense for you after you confirm.
