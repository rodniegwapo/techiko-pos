1. In the sidebar, open **Finance → Expenses** and go to the **Recurring** tab.
2. Click **Add Recurring Expense**.
3. Enter the **Amount**, **Category**, **Description** (e.g. "Shop rent") and, if you like, the **Payee** and **Store**.
4. Choose **Paid from** and how it **Repeats**: **Monthly** (pick the **Day of the month**) or **Weekly** (pick the **Day of the week**).
5. Set when it **Starts** and, if it stops, when it **Ends (optional)**. Save.

Each time it comes due, the expense is recorded for you. Use **Pause** / **Resume** on the row to stop it for a while. You can also tick **Repeat** when recording a normal expense.
