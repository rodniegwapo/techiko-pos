1. In the sidebar, open **Finance → VAT report**.
2. Choose the period (and store, if needed). The report shows the VAT collected on sales.
3. Click **Export Excel** to download it.

VAT is only collected when **Apply VAT automatically on sales** is on under **Settings**.
