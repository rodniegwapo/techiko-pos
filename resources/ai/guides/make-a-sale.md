1. In the sidebar, open **Sales**.
2. Tap products to add them to the order, or search or scan their barcode. Tap **+** / **−** in the order to change quantities.
3. Optional: tap an item in the order to give it a discount, use **Apply Order Discount** for the whole order, or switch **Walk-in** to **Loyal** to pick a customer.
4. Under **Payment method**, choose **Cash**, **Card**, **Credit** or **Other** (E-wallet, Bank, Split payment).
5. For cash, type the **Amount Received**; the **Change** is shown.
6. Classic screen: click **Proceed Payment** and confirm. Modern screen: click **Checkout**, then pay.
7. The sale is saved and the order clears for the next customer.

Tip: the Classic/Modern switch (top of the order panel) changes how the sales screen looks; both work the same.
