1. In the sidebar, open **Sales**.
2. Tap products to add them to the order, or scan their barcode. Change quantities in the order list.
3. Optional: apply a discount (**Quick Discounts**, **PWD**, **Senior**) or pick a customer for loyalty points or credit.
4. Click **Proceed Payment**, choose the **Payment method** (Cash, Card or another channel) and enter the amount received.
5. Complete the payment. The receipt and change are shown.
