1. On the **Sales** screen, switch the customer from **Walk-in** to **Loyal**.
2. Search the customer by name, phone or email (at least 2 letters) and pick them. If they are new, add them there.
3. Add the products to the order. Loyal customers earn loyalty points on paid sales.
4. To sell on credit (utang), choose **Credit** under **Payment method**. The customer's **Available Credit** and **Current Balance** are shown; it is blocked if the credit is not enough.
5. Click **Proceed Payment** (or **Checkout** on the Modern screen).

Credit only works for customers with credit enabled (see "Set a customer's credit limit"), and only while online.
