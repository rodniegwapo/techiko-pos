key: void-item-in-cart
title: Remove or void an item from the order
page: sales.index
requires: sales.index
keywords: remove item, void item, cancel item, delete item from order, alisin, tanggalin, mali ang item
---
1. On the **Sales** screen, find the item in the order.
2. Click its remove (trash) button (**Remove item from order**).
3. In the void window, enter the **Pin** and a **Reason**, then confirm. A cashier needs a manager's or admin's PIN; managers and admins use their own.
4. The item is removed and the void is recorded in **Void Logs**.

To lower the quantity instead, use the **−** button on the item.
