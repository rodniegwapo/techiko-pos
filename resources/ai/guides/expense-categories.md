1. In the sidebar, open **Finance → Expenses** and click **Record Expense**.
2. Next to **Category**, click **Manage**.
3. In **Expense Categories**, type a **New category name** and click **Add**. Use **Rename** to change one.
4. Close the window; the new category is in the list.

A category that was already used can only be deactivated, not deleted.
