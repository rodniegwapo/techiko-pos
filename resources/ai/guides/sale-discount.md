**One item:**
1. On the **Sales** screen, add the product to the order.
2. Tap that item in the order. The **Apply Discount** window opens.
3. Under **Select Discount**, pick the discount and save.

**The whole order:**
1. Next to **Order Discount** (in the totals area), click the discount icon (**Apply Order Discount**).
2. Choose a promotional discount, and/or a mandatory one (PWD, Senior, Student).
3. Save. The **Order Discount** amount and **Total** update.

Discounts you can pick are set up under **Products → Discounts** (and **Products → Mandatory Discounts** for PWD/Senior).
