1. In the sidebar, open **Products → Discounts**.
2. Click **Create Discount**.
3. Enter the **Discount Name**, choose the **Discount Type** (**Percentage** or **Amount**) and the **Discount Value**.
4. Choose the **Scope**: the whole order, or one item.
5. Optional: a **Minimum Order Amount**, and a **Start Date** and **End Date** for a limited-time promo.
6. Save. Cashiers can now pick it on the Sales screen.

The assistant can also create or change a discount for you after you confirm.
