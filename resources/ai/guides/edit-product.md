title: Change a product's price or details
page: products.index
requires: products.edit, products.update

---
1. In the sidebar, open **Products → Items**.
2. Use **Search products** to find it.
3. Open the product's actions and choose **Edit**.
4. Change the fields you need (name, price, cost, category, SKU, barcode, display style).
5. Save. The new price shows on the POS screen right away.
