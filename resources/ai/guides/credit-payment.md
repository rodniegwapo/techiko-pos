page: credits.index
requires: credits.index, credits.transactions.store
keywords: record payment, credit payment, utang payment, bayad sa utang, nagbayad, collect, collection, pay balance
---
1. In the sidebar, open **Credit Management**.
2. Find the customer and click **Record payment** on their row (or open them and click **Record Payment**).
3. Enter the **Amount** and choose the **Payment Method** (Cash, Card, E-Wallet or Bank).
4. Optional: under **Apply to Invoices (Optional)** pick which unpaid invoices it pays, and add a **Reference Number** and **Notes**.
5. Save. The customer's **Current Balance** goes down and the payment shows in **Payment History**.
