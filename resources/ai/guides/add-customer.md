1. In the sidebar, open **Customers**.
2. Click **Add Customer**.
3. Enter the **Full Name**, and if you have them the **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, **Address** and **Date of Birth**.
4. Tick **Enroll customer in loyalty program** if they should earn points.
5. Save.

To change a customer later, find them with the search box and edit them from their row. You can also add a customer on the **Sales** screen (switch to **Loyal**, then add a new customer).
