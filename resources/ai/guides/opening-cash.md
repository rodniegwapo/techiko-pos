1. In the sidebar, open **Cash & wallet → Money movement**.
2. Under **Cash control**, check the **Business date** (today) and click **Load**.
3. Click **Set opening cash**, enter the cash in the drawer at the start of the day (a suggestion from the last count may be shown), and click **Save opening**.

The expected cash for the day starts from this amount, plus cash sales and cash in, minus cash out and cash expenses.
