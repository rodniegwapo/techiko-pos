1. In the sidebar, open **Cash & wallet → Today's drawer**.
2. Click **Set opening**, enter the cash in the drawer at the start of the day (a suggestion from the last count may be shown), and click **Save opening**.

The expected cash for the day starts from this amount, plus cash sales and cash in, minus cash out (cash expenses included).
