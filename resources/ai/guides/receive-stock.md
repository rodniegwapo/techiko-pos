1. In the sidebar, open **Inventory → Products**.
2. Click **Receive Inventory**.
3. Choose the location receiving the stock.
4. Optional: **Reference Type** (leave empty for Purchase) and **Reference ID** (e.g. the delivery or invoice number).
5. Under **Add Products**, search each product and set its **Quantity**, **Unit Cost** and, if needed, **Expiry Date**.
6. Click **Receive Inventory**. The stock goes up and a movement is recorded.

Entering the unit cost keeps the average cost and profit reports correct. The assistant can receive a delivery into your current store for you after you confirm.
