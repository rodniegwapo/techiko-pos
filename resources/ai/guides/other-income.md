1. In the sidebar, open **Finance → Other income**.
2. Click **Record income**.
3. Enter the **Date**, **Amount** and **What is it?** (e.g. "Rent from stall space", "supplier rebate").
4. Optional: how it was received and a **Reference (optional)**.
5. Click **Save**. It counts in the income statement as other income, not as sales.
