1. In the sidebar, open **Servicing payment**.
2. Check **Current usage** and compare the plans under **Choose plan**.
3. Pick a plan, then pay:
   - **Pay with QR Ph**: scan the code with your bank or e-wallet app, or
   - **Pay with GCash**: send exactly the amount shown, then **Submit reference** with the GCash reference number.
4. Paid plans unlock their limits once the payment is verified.

If you can't add more products or users ("limit reached"), this is where to upgrade.
