1. In the sidebar, open **Inventory → Locations**.
2. Click **Add Location**.
3. Under **Basic Information**, enter the **Location Name**, a short **Location Code** (up to 10 characters, e.g. MAIN or BR2) and the **Location Type** (Store or Warehouse).
4. Optional: the **Address**, and under **Contact Information** the contact person, phone number and email.
5. Click **Create Location** and confirm.
