1. In the sidebar, open **Inventory → Stock Adjustments**.
2. Click **New Adjustment**.
3. Under **Basic Information**, choose the location and the reason (e.g. **Physical Count**, **Damaged Goods**, **Expired Goods**, **Theft/Loss**).
4. Search and add the products. For each, type the **Actual Qty** you counted; the **Adjustment** is worked out from the **System Qty**.
5. Click **Save Adjustment**. It is saved as a draft and does not change stock yet.
6. In the list, click **Submit for approval**. Stock changes only after a manager approves it.
