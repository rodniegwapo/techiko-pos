page: sales.index

1. Open **Sales**.
2. Find the **Classic / Modern** switch (in the order panel under **Layout**, or next to the title on a phone).
3. Turn it on for **Modern** (big product pictures, **Checkout** button) or off for **Classic** (list with totals at the bottom and **Proceed Payment**).

Both layouts work the same way and keep the same order. Your choice is remembered on this device.
