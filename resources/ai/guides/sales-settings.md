1. In the sidebar, open **Settings**.
2. Under **Sales**:
   - **Apply VAT automatically on sales**: turn on to add VAT to every sale.
   - **VAT pricing mode**: whether your prices already include VAT (inclusive) or VAT is added on top (exclusive).
   - **VAT rate (%)**: usually 12.
   - **Allow selling when out of stock at the location**: lets cashiers sell items with no stock left.
   - **Hide out-of-stock products at the POS**: hides items with no stock from the sales screen.
3. Click **Save**. Carts not yet paid are re-totalled with the new VAT setting.
