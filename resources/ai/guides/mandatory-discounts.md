1. In the sidebar, open **Products → Mandatory Discounts**.
2. Click **Create Mandatory Discount** (or edit an existing one).
3. Enter the **Discount Name** (e.g. Senior Citizen, PWD, Student), the **Discount Type** and the **Discount Value** (e.g. 20 for 20%).
4. Set the **Status** to active and click **Submit**.

Cashiers apply these through **Apply Order Discount** on the Sales screen.
