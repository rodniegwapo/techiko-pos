title: Find a past sale or receipt
page: sales-history.index
requires: sales-history.index, sales-history.show
keywords: receipt, invoice, past sale, find sale, sales history, reprint, resibo, lumang benta, transaction history
---
1. In the sidebar, open **Sales History**.
2. Type the **Invoice #** or the customer's name in the search box, or use the filters (date range, location, cashier).
3. Click the sale to open it. You see the items, discounts, VAT, payment, cashier and profit.
4. Click **Reprint receipt** to print it again.

Cashiers who may only see their own sales see **My sales** instead of all sales.
