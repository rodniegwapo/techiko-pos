# Cash & wallet — how it works, by role

Narration for the explainer video. One `## scene-N` per voiceover clip: `tts.mjs` reads the
**Say:** paragraph of each scene; **Show:** is what the screen does meanwhile. Keep lines short
and plain: they are read aloud.

## scene-1 — Cash & wallet at a glance
**Show:** title card, then the five tabs sliding in: Today's drawer, Where money is, Cash in & out, Shifts & counts, Cash on hand.

**Say:** This is Cash and wallet. It tracks every peso in your business: the cash in each store's drawer, the money in your payment channels like GCash or a card terminal, and the cash the owner holds, called Cash on hand. It has five tabs, and what you see depends on your role.

## scene-2 — Roles
**Show:** four role cards side by side: Cashier, Supervisor, Manager, Owner or Admin, each with its tabs.

**Say:** There are four roles. A cashier handles their own shift. A supervisor watches the store's whole drawer and checks the cashiers' counts. A manager ends the store's day. And the owner, or an admin, sees every store and is the only one who moves money between stores.

## scene-3 — Cashier
**Show:** Sales → Start shift with opening cash → a sale → Today's drawer → My shift → End shift, count the tray.

**Say:** As a cashier, start your shift from Sales, or from Today's drawer under My shift, and enter the cash you start with. Sell as usual. When you are done, end your shift and count your own tray. You don't need a PIN. If your count is short or over, it waits for a supervisor to check it.

## scene-4 — Supervisor
**Show:** Today's drawer (whole store) → set opening cash → spot check → Shifts & counts → Approve, and Send back for a recount.

**Say:** A supervisor sees the store's whole drawer. Set the opening cash, and do a spot check any time to see if the drawer matches. On Shifts and counts you see your team's shifts. Approve a count, or send it back to the cashier for a recount.

## scene-5 — Manager
**Show:** Today's drawer → End the day, count, choose Cash out or Keep as opening → Cash in & out → a line between the drawer and a channel.

**Say:** A manager does all that for their own store, and ends the store's day once every cashier has ended their shift. Count the drawer, then choose. Cash out sends the money to Cash on hand. Keep as opening leaves it in the drawer for tomorrow. On Cash in and out, a manager records money in and out, and moves money between the drawer and the store's own channels. A manager can't send money to another store.

## scene-6 — Owner setup
**Show:** Inventory → Locations → edit a store → turn on Can transfer money → same for the second store.

**Say:** Moving money between stores is for the owner or an admin. First, turn on Can transfer money for both stores, under Inventory, Locations. Also, neither store's day can be ended for that date.

## scene-7 — Three ways to move money to another store
**Show:** diagram. Way 1: Store A → Store B, direct arrow. Way 2: Store A drawer → owner → Store B drawer, direct. Way 3: Store A → Cash on hand → Store B. Then the app for each.

**Say:** There are three ways to move money to another store, and only one of them goes through Cash on hand. One: Transfer, on Cash in and out. Pick the other store, and the money goes straight there, drawer or channel. Two: Owner draw, put into another store's drawer. That is also direct, and only from the cash drawer. Three: through Cash on hand. The owner collects an approved shift's cash, or the manager cashes out at the end of the day. It sits in Cash on hand. Then, on Cash on hand, choose To store, and pick the store's drawer or one of its channels.

## scene-8 — Which one to use
**Show:** table: Need it there now → Transfer. Taking cash with you to the other store → Owner draw. Collecting cash first, deciding later → Cash on hand.

**Say:** Which one? If the other store needs money now, use Transfer. If you are carrying cash from one drawer to another, use Owner draw. If you collect cash from the stores first and decide later, use Cash on hand. Whatever you choose, it is still the business's money. Only Owner used it, on Cash on hand, takes money out of the business.

## scene-9 — Recap
**Show:** the four role cards again, then the end card.

**Say:** So: cashiers count their own tray, supervisors check the counts, managers end the store's day, and the owner moves money between stores, directly or through Cash on hand. That's Cash and wallet.
